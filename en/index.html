Welcome

A home in the city's most beautiful quarter

Merì is a small, family-run bed & breakfast in the heart of Castello, the fortified quarter overlooking Cagliari from above. Just a few rooms, each with a private bathroom, and a lot of care: we welcome you personally, share our favourite restaurants and sights, and leave you free to feel at home.

Step out of the front door and you're already in the most authentic Cagliari: artisan workshops, panoramic terraces over the gulf, historic cafés — and Poetto beach just fifteen minutes away.

Getting here

Airport, train and car

Castello is a limited-traffic zone (ZTL): you can't drive up to the front door. Here are the easiest ways to reach us.

  • ✈️
    From the airport by train From Elmas Aeroporto station, a short walk from the terminal, the train takes you in about 10 minutes to Cagliari station in Piazza Matteotti (ticket about €1.30, a train roughly every 20 minutes).
  • 🚌
    Then bus no. 7 In front of the station, take CTM bus line 7: it climbs up to Castello and stops right outside our door.
  • 🅿️
    Coming by car? Here's where to park Castello is a ZTL: leave the car outside the quarter. The nearest free parking is on Viale Buoncammino; paid options are the street areas and the multi-storey car park on Viale Regina Elena, linked to Castello by public lifts. Read our full parking guide, with maps and prices →
  • 🚐
    Transfer on request Rather be picked up? We can arrange a transfer to and from the property: just ask us in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

How do I get there from the airport?

By train from Elmas Aeroporto to Cagliari station in Piazza Matteotti (about 10 minutes), then CTM bus line 7, which stops right outside our door.

Is Castello a ZTL? Where can I park?

Yes, Castello is a limited-traffic zone: please don't drive into the quarter. The nearest free parking is on Viale Buoncammino; paid options are the street areas and the multi-storey car park on Viale Regina Elena, linked to Castello by public lifts. All the details, with maps and prices, are in our parking guide: https://www.meribnb.it/en/parking/

Is a transfer available?

Yes, on request: write to us in advance to arrange it.

Is the tap water drinkable?

Yes, the tap water is drinkable: fresh and good to drink.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM; just let us know if you need a different time. Check-out is by 10:30 AM. You can complete online check-in in advance using the button at the top of the page.

Do the rooms have a private bathroom?

Yes, every room has a private bathroom with shower, hairdryer and toiletries.

How far is the beach?

Poetto beach, 8 km of white sand, is about 6 km away: 15 minutes by bus or 10 by car.

Are pets allowed?

Unfortunately we cannot accommodate pets at the moment.

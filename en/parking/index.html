Parking guide · Castello, Cagliari

Where to park
near Castello

Castello is a limited-traffic zone (ZTL): visitors' cars can't enter the quarter. The good news is that just outside the walls there are free and paid car parks, linked to the B&B by short walks and public lifts. Here's everything you need, with maps and prices.

🧭
Before you set off: put the car park in your sat-nav, not the B&B address

If you enter Via Alberto Lamarmora 18, your sat-nav may take you into the ZTL, where cameras issue fines. Pick one of the car parks below and use the «Walking route» button for the last stretch.

In short: which one to choose

  • 💸 Spend nothing Viale Buoncammino: free white-line spaces, about a 10-minute, mostly flat walk.
  • 🪙 Spend little Regina Elena Alta area: €2.50 per day maximum, then lifts up to Castello.
  • 🛡️ Covered and open 24/7 Regina Elena multi-storey car park: about €1.70 per hour, €10 per day maximum.

Useful tips

A few tips for a stress-free arrival.

  • 🧭
    Sat-nav

    Set the car park you've chosen as your destination, not the B&B address: that way you won't drive into the ZTL by mistake.

  • 🚫
    ZTL and cameras

    Don't drive into Castello: the entrances are monitored by cameras. The spaces in Piazza Indipendenza are reserved for residents too.

  • 🌙
    Arriving in the evening or at the weekend

    Blue lines are free on Sundays, public holidays and Monday to Saturday from 8 PM to 9 AM and from 1 to 4 PM.

  • 🌡️
    Lifts not running?

    In summer the lifts may stop during the hottest hours. You can walk up along Via Ubaldo Badas, or park on Viale Buoncammino, which is already at Castello's level.

  • 🧳
    Heavy luggage

    We can arrange a transfer on request: write to us before you arrive.
